# Verification checklist (run before every answer)

For each claim and quote in the draft:
- [ ] I fetched the source in this task (not recalled it), and the page says what I claim.
- [ ] Quotes match the fetched text word for word; speaker, title and date are copied from the page.
- [ ] The link resolves and lands on the quoted paragraph (anchored link opened once).
- [ ] The authority label is right (scripture / doctrine / policy / leader's teaching / manual / scholarship /
      speculation), and no single person's view is presented as Church doctrine.
- [ ] Time-sensitive material (policy, handbook wording, current leaders, curriculum) was read today and dated.
- [ ] A secondary source's claim was traced to its primary source when the answer rests on it.
- [ ] Famous quotes: confirmed in the original talk or document. Quotes widely shared online are often
      misattributed or altered; if the original can't be found, say "unverified" or "misattributed", with where
      you searched.
- [ ] Scripture references: book, chapter and verse checked against the fetched chapter text.
- [ ] Anything that failed a check is cut or listed under "What I couldn't verify".

Self-test before sending: could the asker click every number and find the claim within seconds? If not, fix it.
