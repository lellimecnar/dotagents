# Two-week "first paying user" experiment (template)

Use for any solo-built app or SaaS. 10 working days (schedule around the founder’s availability). Built on: Mom Test conversations and commitment [customer-discovery.md], manual recruiting [PG-DS https://paulgraham.com/ds.html], payment link and fees [launch-channels.md, https://stripe.com/payments/payment-links, https://stripe.com/pricing], pricing rules [positioning-pricing.md], ADHD work methods [adhd-founder-methods.md]. Thresholds below are working decision rules, not sourced benchmarks.

## Before day 1: facts to get (from the project's own status source)
1. Can a stranger reach and use the product today, at a URL or in a store? If not, what is the smallest fix and how long?
2. Can it take money today (payment link, in-app purchase, invoice)?
3. Who already uses it for free, and can we email them?
4. Which single feature do current users rely on most?
If the product is not reachable: run the same plan selling a **pre-sale / founding membership with a refund promise** or a **manual (concierge) version** of the core value. Do not wait for infrastructure.

## Day 1: decide (60-90 min)
- One segment as a who-and-where pair.
- One hypothesis: "[segment] currently handles [problem] with [alternative], costing them [time/money]; at least 1 of them will pay $[price]/month for [value] within 10 working days."
- Price: a clean number below the cost of their alternative; one plan, monthly + annual; founding price optional.
- Kill/continue rule written down (see below).
- Accountability: name the check-in partner and the two check-in days (day 5 and day 10).
- First physical action for day 2 written as an if-then plan.

## Day 2: list and link
- Write 30 named prospects (people or places where the segment gathers), including every existing free user reachable by email.
- Prepare (do not activate without the owner's go-ahead) a hosted payment link for the plan. Verify the approved processor’s current fees and conditions.

## Days 3-4: learn (Mom Test)
- Send 10 written messages a day (outreach skeleton in launch-channels.md). No pitching.
- Every conversation: last time the problem happened, current workaround, what it costs, who else to talk to. Log each row.
- End each conversation with a small commitment (follow-up time, trying a manual version with their data, an introduction).

## Day 5: check-in 1 (30 min, with the partner)
Count: messages, replies, conversations, commitments. Decide:
- 0 conversations show a real past workaround or spend → change segment now (keep the method, new who-and-where).
- Pain confirmed → go to the offer.

## Days 6-9: ask for money
- Send the offer to everyone who showed real pain: value in their words, price, refund promise, link.
- Existing free users: one email with the paid offer and the founding price.
- If the product can be tried and the audience is technical, one Show HN that follows its rules (no landing pages).
- Deliver manually where needed (grant access by hand, do the service yourself).
- Follow up once, in writing, with everyone who didn't answer.

## Day 10: check-in 2 and decision (45 min)
| Result | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1+ payment | Continue: deliver superbly, ask the payer "who else should I talk to?", repeat the cycle for payment #2-#5; start weekly payment tracking (unit-economics.md) |
| 0 payments but 3+ money-level commitments (deposit, LOI, "bill me when it's live" with a date) | One more 2-week cycle focused on removing the stated blocker |
| Commitments only at time level | Price or value mismatch: rerun with a narrower segment or an outcome-based offer |
| Fewer than 10 real conversations happened | Execution problem, not market verdict: shrink daily targets, add check-ins, rerun |
| Clear evidence nobody in reachable segments spends on this problem | Stop this product line or pivot the problem; write down why |

## Daily scoreboard (one line per day)
Date | messages sent | replies | conversations | commitments (time/reputation/money) | payments | MRR | next physical action

## Rules for the whole two weeks
- No new features unless a person who has committed money is blocked by their absence.
- No rebuilds, migrations, redesigns.
- Spending of any kind goes to the owner first, with the free alternative.
- Each day ends with something sent or asked.
