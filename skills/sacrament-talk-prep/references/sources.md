# Sources behind this skill (verified 2026-11-16; re-fetch before quoting)

Guidance on sacrament meeting talks
- General Handbook, 29.2.1.1 "Planning Sacrament Meeting" and 29.2.1.4 "Selecting Speakers":
  https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/29-meetings-in-the-church?lang=eng
  (focus on the sacrament and building faith in Jesus Christ; speakers bear testimony of Him and teach His gospel
  using the scriptures, citing D&C 42:12; 52:9; messages consistent with the sacred nature of the sacrament; visual
  aids and audiovisual materials not used, see 38.8.3; missionaries speaking, see 24.5.2 and 24.8.3).
- "Speaking in Sacrament Meeting?", New Era, Oct. 2011 (four elements; start early; prayer, D&C 42:14; outline over
  reading, D&C 100:6; Hinckley "speak out of [our] hearts"):
  https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/new-era/2011/10/speaking-in-sacrament-meeting?lang=eng
- "7 Tips for Giving a Talk in Sacrament Meeting", New Era, July 2018 (scriptures and modern prophets, personal
  story, practice aloud, avoid the "I don't want to be up here" opener, speak clearly, look up):
  https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/new-era/2018/07/7-tips-for-giving-a-talk-in-sacrament-meeting?lang=eng
- "Improving Sacrament Meeting Worship: 90 Practical Ideas" (Church PDF, 2026; linked from a Church area
  page "Preparing Your Talk"): items 52-70 cover speakers: Christ-centered topics, avoid book-report talks,
  practice aloud, stay within time, remove fluff, scripture-based and doctrinally pure, talks about 6-8 minutes.
  https://assets.churchofjesuschrist.org/43/tz/43tzgwlke5rjnno300k6wh2w5vwfdi155bx9jro9/90_practical_ideas_to_enhance_and_strengthen_your_sabbath_day_experience.pdf
  It is area/training material, not the Handbook: say so when citing it; the bishopric's allotted time governs.
