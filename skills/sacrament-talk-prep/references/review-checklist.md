# Outline review checklist

Content
- [ ] One clear doctrine, stated early, explicitly connected to Jesus Christ and His Atonement.
- [ ] Each scripture teaches the point it is placed under; references checked.
- [ ] Every quote verified word for word, with speaker, talk and date.
- [ ] No speculation, folk doctrine or personal interpretation presented as doctrine.
- [ ] A starting conference talk (if any) supports the topic rather than being summarized ("book report").
- [ ] Includes the speaker's own experience or witness, not only borrowed stories.
- [ ] Ends with testimony of Jesus Christ and a clear, kind invitation.

Fit for sacrament meeting
- [ ] Reverent, consistent with the sacred nature of the sacrament.
- [ ] No visual aids or audiovisual materials (Handbook 38.8.3).
- [ ] Nothing that embarrasses others, discloses private family matters, or criticizes members or leaders.
- [ ] Opening goes straight in (no "I didn't want to be up here").

Time
- [ ] Section minutes sum to the allotted time with a little slack; a cut item is named.
- [ ] Suggest one read-through aloud from the outline against a timer.
