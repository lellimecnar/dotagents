# When to refer out: emergency and licensed-professional routes

Research informs a decision; it does not replace emergency services or a licensed professional. Give the
evidence summary, then the route. Offer a safe, supported channel; in emergencies, prioritize the fastest safe local route.

## Emergency routes (US)
| Situation | Route | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Life, health or safety in danger now | 911. Text-to-911 exists only in some areas; FCC: call if you can, text if you can't; give location first | FCC text-to-911 guide (as quoted by UC Irvine Emergency Management) [1] |
| Emotional crisis, suicidal thoughts, substance crisis, worry about someone | 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call, text or chat at 988lifeline.org, 24/7, free, confidential | 988 Lifeline [2] |
| Possible poisoning, person awake with no life-threatening symptoms | webPOISONCONTROL online triage (web or app); it says when a poison centre or ER is needed | Poison Control [3] |
| Collapse, seizure, trouble breathing, can't be woken after exposure | 911 immediately | Poison Control [3] |
| Suspected intentional poisoning | Seek emergency medical care now; do not use a web triage tool or wait for a lookup. | [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/need-immediate-assistance) |
| Other suspected human poisoning when online eligibility is uncertain or the case is excluded | Contact the local poison centre immediately (U.S. example: 1-800-222-1222). Use online triage only when the provider's current criteria clearly apply; do not delay an urgent referral to check them. | [Poison Control](https://www.poison.org/need-immediate-assistance) |
| Suspected pet poisoning | Contact a veterinarian or animal poison service promptly; do not use human web triage. | Poison Control [3] |

## Licensed-professional triggers
Stop at the evidence summary and name the professional when the question needs:
- Diagnosis, dosing, or a change to medication: clinician or pharmacist (patient portal message first).
- Legal advice on a live matter, contracts with real stakes, deadlines with penalties: attorney; state
  bar referral services typically have web forms.
- Tax filing positions or audits: enrolled agent or CPA; IRS online account for records.
- Structural, gas, electrical, or permit-bound work: licensed contractor; check the state licence lookup.
- Investments or insurance decisions with large sums: fee-only fiduciary adviser.
These categories are standard practice; the specific routes vary by state, so verify the current page
before pointing anyone to it.

## Channel preferences
Use an accessible channel the requester prefers when practical. If a process truly needs an excluded channel, say so plainly and suggest who
else could use it.
