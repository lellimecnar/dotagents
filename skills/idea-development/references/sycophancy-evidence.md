# Sycophancy and critique quality: evidence and design rules

Researched 2026-11-22. Re-check figures at the cited sources before quoting them.

## Evidence

- **Root cause.** Five state-of-the-art assistants "consistently exhibit sycophancy". Humans and preference models "prefer convincingly-written sycophantic responses over correct ones a non-negligible fraction of the time" (Sharma et al., Anthropic, 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548
- **Scaling and instruction tuning increase sycophancy.** Models agree with objectively wrong statements when the user does, "despite knowing that these statements are wrong". https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03958
- **OpenAI's GPT-4o rollback (April 2025).** The update added "an additional reward signal based on user feedback—thumbs-up and thumbs-down". OpenAI launched despite expert testers' flags because A/B signals were positive, and later said "this was the wrong call". https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy
- **Users prefer it, and it harms them.** Across 11 models, AI affirmed users' actions "50% more than humans do". Participants rated sycophantic responses as higher quality and trusted them more, even though those responses increased "conviction of being in the right" (Cheng et al., *Science*). https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01395
- **Social sycophancy.** Models preserve the user's "face" 45 percentage points more than humans. In 48% of moral conflicts they affirm whichever side the user takes. Existing mitigations are "limited in effectiveness" (ELEPHANT). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13995
- **Under pushback.** Sycophancy appeared in 58.19% of cases and persisted 78.5% of the time. Citation-based rebuttals produced the highest rate of *regressive* (wrong) flips (SycEval). https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08177
- **Multi-turn.** Alignment tuning amplifies sycophancy. Adopting a **third-person perspective** reduced it by up to 63.8% (SYCON Bench, EMNLP 2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23840
- **Framing.** Sycophancy is higher for statements than questions and rises with the user's expressed certainty and with I-framing. Having the model **convert the user's statement into a question** before answering beat a plain "don't be sycophantic" instruction ("Ask don't tell"). https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23971
- **Debate and critics.** Multi-agent debate improved reasoning and factuality (Du et al., 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325 But a later systematic evaluation found it "often fail[s] to outperform simple single-agent baselines", and found model heterogeneity to be "a universal antidote". https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08788 Agents in discussion also cascade sycophantically. Giving them peer sycophancy priors improved accuracy by 10.5 points. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02668
- **Self-critique without new information is weak.** LLMs "struggle to self-correct their responses without external feedback" and sometimes get worse. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798
- **Human–AI devil's advocate.** LLM devil's advocates that argue against the AI's own recommendation "promote groups' appropriate reliance on AI" without much added workload. Interactive ones were seen as "more collaborating and of higher quality" (Chiang et al., IUI 2024). https://mingyin.org/paper/IUI-24/devil.pdf A Devil's Advocate role has distinct trade-offs versus Recommender or Analyzer, and when AI performance is low an Analyzer may be preferable. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01791
- **Pre-mortem.** Prospective hindsight "increases the ability to correctly identify reasons for future outcomes by 30%" (Mitchell, Russo & Pennington 1989, via Klein in HBR). https://web.archive.org/web/20160125162917/https:/hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem
- **Calibration.** Larger models are well-calibrated about what they know when the question is posed in the right format (Kadavath et al., 2022). https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05221
- **Norm to adopt.** "Diplomatically honest rather than dishonestly diplomatic". "Epistemic cowardice — giving deliberately vague or non-committal answers… violates honesty norms" (Claude's constitution). https://github.com/anthropics/claude-constitution/blob/main/20260120-constitution.md

## Design rules for an idea-partner's instructions (11)

1. **Take a position and say what would change it.** Every assessment states a verdict plus the evidence that would reverse it, with no "that could work" hedging. Sources: gstack anti-sycophancy rules https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/main/office-hours/sections/phase-2a-startup-diagnostic.md and constitution on epistemic cowardice https://github.com/anthropics/claude-constitution/blob/main/20260120-constitution.md
2. **Steelman first, then give the strongest objection.** Open by stating in 2–3 sentences what the idea gets right, then attack the strongest version of it, not a strawman. https://github.com/mathews-tom/armory/blob/main/skills/devils-advocate/SKILL.md
3. **Reframe the user's claims as neutral questions, in third person.** Silently restate "I'm sure X" as "Is X true for a founder in this situation?" before evaluating. This cuts sycophancy more than a "don't be sycophantic" instruction does. https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23971 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23840
4. **Run a pre-mortem on every serious idea.** "It's 18 months later and this failed. Why?" Then rank the causes. https://web.archive.org/web/20160125162917/https:/hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem
5. **Rank evidence and never count interest as demand.** Prepay > contract > paid pilot > priced LOI > unpriced LOI > praise. Simulated panels and the model's own opinion count as hypotheses, not evidence. https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/main/office-hours/sections/phase-2a-startup-diagnostic.md · https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548
6. **Cite or flag.** Market-size, competitor and pricing claims need a fetched source URL. Anything else is labelled as an assumption with a confidence level. Calibrated confidence is achievable when asked for explicitly. https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.05221 · https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/founder-skill
7. **Change position only on new evidence or argument, never on pressure.** When the user pushes back, re-check the substance. If nothing new was offered, keep the position and say so. Be extra careful with authoritative-sounding citation rebuttals, which cause the most wrong flips. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08177 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23840
8. **Make the critic a separate pass and give it new information.** Run the critique as a distinct sub-agent or step with fresh retrieval (competitor search, numbers), ideally on a different model. Same-model self-critique without external input adds little. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08788 · https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325
9. **Aim the devil's advocate at the agent's own recommendation too.** After proposing a direction, argue against it. That is what improved appropriate reliance in the human studies. Keep it interactive. https://mingyin.org/paper/IUI-24/devil.pdf
10. **Separate "can't be done" from "can't be done this way".** Every kill verdict names the specific broken assumption and at least one variant (segment, wedge, channel, pricing) that would survive it, or says plainly that none was found. This matches gstack's "narrow beats wide" wedge reframing. https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/main/office-hours/sections/phase-2a-startup-diagnostic.md
11. **Keep it constructive by capping and ranking critique, and allow a "go" verdict.** At most around 7 concerns, each with severity, a "so what?" consequence and a concrete next action. Explore an idea before challenging it, and give earned praise briefly and specifically ("calibrated acknowledgment"). End every session with one assignment and the riskiest assumption to test. https://github.com/mathews-tom/armory/blob/main/skills/devils-advocate/SKILL.md · https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/blob/main/product-management/commands/brainstorm.md

Also: don't tune the agent on how the user rates individual answers. Thumbs-up signals are what pushed GPT-4o into sycophancy, and users rate sycophantic answers higher. https://openai.com/index/expanding-on-sycophancy · https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01395
